Welcome to PixelDrop
====================

PixelDrop is the internal file drop for the PixelForge design team.
Upload a small text asset, tag it public or private, and share the
download link with the rest of the studio.

Public drops are listed on the Files page and can be downloaded by
anyone who has the link. Private drops are only listed for their owner.
